## 📱 3 SIMPLE STEPS:

### Step 1: Install Expo Go (1 minute)
1. Open **Google Play Store** on your Android phone
2. Search: **"Expo Go"**
3. Install (free, ~50MB)

### Step 2: Connect to WiFi (30 seconds)
- Make sure your **phone** and **computer** are on the **SAME WiFi network**

### Step 3: Scan QR Code (30 seconds)
1. Look at your terminal/command prompt
2. Find the **QR code** (big square pattern)
3. Open **Expo Go** app
4. Tap **"Scan QR Code"**
5. Point camera at QR code
6. Wait 10-30 seconds

### 🎉 DONE! Your app is now running on your phone!

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### "Can't connect to Metro"
→ Check both devices are on same WiFi

### "Network timeout"
→ Try tunnel mode:
```powershell
npx expo start --tunnel
```

### "QR code won't scan"
→ In Expo Go app, tap "Enter URL manually"
→ Type the URL shown in terminal (e.g., `exp://192.168.1.7:8081`)
